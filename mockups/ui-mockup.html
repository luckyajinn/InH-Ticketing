InH-Ticketing

Report an IT problem or ask for access or equipment, then follow every update from the help desk in one place.

Sign in with your Microsoft 365 work account

You sign in on your company's Microsoft 365 page. InH-Ticketing never sees or stores your password.

Can't sign in? Call the IT desk on ext. 4357 or email it-help@example.com.

Design noteOne button, no password field, no sign-up and no reset flow. Entra ID handles it all over OIDC. Role (Requester, Agent, Admin) comes from Entra group membership, so everyone lands on the right home screen.
