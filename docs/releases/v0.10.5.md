# v0.10.5: The admin log, and the admin's ticket page fixed

**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Built on:** `main`

Two changes for admins: a record of everything done in the admin site, and a
layout fix.

## The admin log

**Administration → Log entries** lists every change made in the admin site,
newest first: when, who, the action, the type of thing, which one, and the
details. For example:

> Nov. 12, 2026, 10:09 a.m. · Riley Chen · Changed · Department · Mechanics
>
> Changed Alert address: blank → "mechanics-team@example.com".

- **The values, not only the fields.** A change to a department, a category
  (its common requests too) or a person says what each field was and what it
  became. Adding a department or a category lists what it was given. A long
  value, such as a list of common requests, is cut at 200 characters.
- **Filters** by action (added, changed, deleted), by person, by type
  (Department, Category, User, Outbound email…) and by date, and a search.
- **Read-only.** Nobody can add, change or delete an entry there, admins
  included.
- **"Retry selected emails now"** on *Outbound emails* is logged too, one
  entry per email. Before, it left no trace.
- **People can't be deleted in the admin site any more.** Deleting someone
  would have deleted their log entries with them. Untick **Active** to block
  someone instead. (People with tickets or replies already couldn't be
  deleted.)

Changes made in the admin site before v0.10.5 are in the log too, naming the
fields that changed but without the values.

Where the rest is recorded:

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Ticket changes, by anyone (status, priority, assignee, category, department moves, the archive, removals) | The ticket's history: on its page in the app, and *Ticket events* on its page in the admin site |
| Password warnings | Admin → *Password warnings* |
| Emails sent and received | Admin → *Outbound emails* and *Inbound emails* |
| Role assignments and sign-ins | Entra's *Audit logs* and *Sign-in logs* |

Not recorded anywhere: who opened or read a ticket.

## The admin's ticket page: no more overlapping lines

On a ticket's page in the admin site, each comment, history line and file
starts with a title line, such as "Reply on #1002 by Sam Patel". It sat on
top of the date and the text. It's now above them, on a phone and on a
computer.

## Upgrading

Follow [deployment.md](../deployment.md), section 10, with `v0.10.5`. No
migrations, and nothing to do in `.env`. Rolling back to v0.10.4 is a
checkout and rebuild.

## Tests

453 automated tests, all passing. The new ones cover: a department added
and changed (the values logged), saved without changes, a category's
department and common requests (names, on one line, long lists cut), a
person deactivated, people not deletable, retrying emails, the log page and
its filters, a deleted item not linked, nothing in the log addable,
changeable or deletable, and only admins seeing it. Checked in the browser:
the log page after a real change, at phone and desktop widths; the ticket
page's comments and history at both widths. Not tried on the server.
