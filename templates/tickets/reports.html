How these are counted. First response: the first public reply from someone other than the requester, or the request being resolved by someone else. Time to resolve: from the request to its resolution, not counting time waiting on the requester. Both are medians in clock time, nights and weekends included. Reopened: put back to Open by the requester or IT (a reply to a resolved request isn't counted). Blocking: the share of web requests that ticked "This is blocking my work" (emails can't say).
